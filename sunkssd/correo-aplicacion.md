## Walkthrough escrito — Kit Hotel agent system (adjunto en el correo)

**The problem:** a 50-room hotel in Mexico City ran check-in on paper and answered reviews by hand. Guests waited up to 20 minutes at reception; reviews sat unanswered.

**What I built (Base44 + n8n + WhatsApp):**

1. **Digital check-in** — the guest completes registration on their phone in \~30 seconds; the data lands in the hotel's system, no front-desk queue.
2. **AI review agent** — reads each new review, drafts a response in the hotel's tone, and posts it after a human approval step.
3. **WhatsApp campaigns** — stay and guest data trigger segmented campaigns automatically (126 messages sent, 10 paid reservations — closed and collected by the hotel team).

**How the agents collaborate:** check-in data feeds the guest record; the review agent watches new reviews; the campaign agent uses stay history to pick who gets which message and when. Every step logs what happened and errors route to a human — nothing sends without a record.

**What I'd do at SUNKSSD:** the same pattern, applied to your agency — specialized agents for research, content and reporting, connected to your CRM and tools, with a command center showing tasks, agent activity and progress, and human approvals where they matter.
